## Project Overview

This project analyzes ApnoMart's sales data to understand sales performance, profitability, regional trends, and customer purchasing behavior. An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to help identify business opportunities and support data-driven decisions.

## Key Business Insights

1. **Sales Trends:** July recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately 2.96 million. October sales were approximately 2.47 million through October 28.
2. **Profitability:** Office Supplies had the highest average profit margin at 34.51%, while Electronics had the lowest at 18.62%.
3. **Customer and Payment Insights:** Corporate customers contributed approximately 4.31 million in sales. Cash was the most frequently used payment method in the dataset.

## Strategic Recommendation

Review Electronics discounting strategies through a controlled pilot and monitor both sales and profit margins. Use the stronger profitability of Office Supplies as a benchmark when evaluating improvements.
